Chaung Thar Beach

Chaung Tha Beach is a popular, lively resort destination in Myanmar's Ayeyarwady Region, known for its affordable, bustling atmosphere, fresh seafood, and proximity to Yangon (about a 5-hour drive). It is a favorite among locals, offering activities like boat trips, horse riding, and visiting the nearby White Sand Island, though it is more crowded and less pristine than neighboring beaches.

Ngwe Saung Beach

Ngwesaung, also spelt Ngwe Hsaung, is a beach resort located 48 km west of Pathein, Ayeyarwady Region, Myanmar. It is the namesake of Ngwesaung Subtownship, Pathein Township. In 2014, the town of Ngwesaung had 10,732 people.

Ngapali Beach

Ngapali Beach is a premier, peaceful, and largely undeveloped beach destination on Myanmar's Rakhine coast, known for its white sand, palm trees, and clear turquoise waters. It serves as a relaxed getaway featuring fresh seafood, snorkeling, and biking, with peak visiting time between October and May, though recent regional tensions have impacted access.
